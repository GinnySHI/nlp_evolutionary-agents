Assumptions

  • The date range is inclusive: bookings on the start date and on the end date are both counted.
  • Capacity = rooms × days in range × 13 hours (07:00–20:00 = 780 minutes per room per day).
  • All calendar days are counted, weekends included; the weekday chart shows weekend effects.
  • Cancelled bookings are counted on their original booking date; their hours appear only as cancelled hours, never as reserved hours or utilisation.
  • Dates are handled as plain YYYY-MM-DD wall-clock values, so no day shifts between local time and UTC.
